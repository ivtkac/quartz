> [!tldr]
> **Structured data** is organized in a clear, defined format, often stored in [[Реляційна база даних|relational database]] or spreadsheets.

> [!note] Can consist of [[Quantitative data|quantitaive]] (sales figures) and [[Qualitative data|qualitative]] data (by category).
