[[The first conditional]] describes a **particular** situation, whereas the [[Zero conditional]] describes what happens **in general**.

> [!example]
> - (0) If you sit in the sun, you get burned (I'm talking about every person, the burning is a natural consequence)
> - (I) If you sit in the sun, you'll get burned (I'm talking about what will happen today, another day may be different)
